WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Methodology & limitations

Heat headlines

IEM's NWS VTEC archive is expanded to local calendar days and the Louisiana LIX forecast zones. Parish/day severity is 0 for none, 1 for advisory and 2 for warning.

LDH surveillance

The automated LDH pull uses the public Heat-Related Illness Tableau dashboard. Daily HRI ED counts are available by LDH region and represent patient residence.

Geography

Regions 1, 2 and 9 align with the Louisiana LIX area used here. LDH Region 3 also contains St. Mary Parish, outside LIX, so South Central comparisons have that known mismatch. Mississippi is excluded.

Interpretation

These are associations, not causal estimates. Weather severity, exposure, behavior, demographics, access to cooling and surveillance/reporting can all influence ED visits.
