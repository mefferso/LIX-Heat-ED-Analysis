WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Methodology & limitations

Headline severity

Each parish/day is scored 0 for no headline, 1 for Heat Advisory, and 2 for Excessive/Extreme Heat Warning. Multi-parish views use the average score across the selected parishes.

LDH surveillance

Heat-related ED visits are grouped by patient parish of residence. LDH reports that roughly 90% of Louisiana emergency departments participate in syndromic surveillance.

Geography

The analysis includes the 22 Louisiana parishes in the LIX CWA. St. Mary is excluded from South Central because it is outside LIX. Mississippi counties are not currently included.

Interpretation

Headline days are not randomized exposure. Weather severity, behavior, access to cooling, demographics, reporting practices, and other factors can affect ED visits.
